What is Lumanta?

A mindfulness app for negative thoughts.

Negative JudgmentalFear You noticed.

An awareness practice that fits in your pocket and takes a single tap.

When you notice a Negative, Judgmental or Fear thought, catch it with one tap. Acknowledge it without judgment or analysis, and practice letting it go.

  • No journaling.
  • No analyzing.
  • No judging yourself.

Over time your patterns come into view. Not as a score, but as awareness. And as awareness grows, you Free Up Space for ideas, creativity, connection and joy.

What could fill that space?

Who is Lumanta for?

For anyone ready to change the way they think.

People come to Lumanta for different reasons, and every one of them is welcome here.

  • A busy mind that won’t settle
  • Worry, stress or mental overload
  • Self-doubt and self-judgment
  • Old patterns of negative thinking
  • A spiritual or mindfulness practice that’s asking for more awareness
  • More focus and room for your ideas

Wherever you’re starting, the practice is the same. Notice the thought. Let it go. Make room for what matters.

You may be wondering.

Lumanta is a mindfulness app for negative thoughts. It follows three steps, Catch, Shift and Rise: you catch a negative, judgmental or fearful thought with one tap, acknowledge it without judgment, and let it go to free up space.

Lumanta helps you notice negative, fearful and judgmental thoughts as they happen, so they don’t quietly run your day. It doesn’t promise you’ll never have a negative thought again. The practice is noticing, and as your awareness grows, you free up space for other ways of thinking.

Many people come to Lumanta because of a busy mind, worry, stress or self-judgment. Lumanta gives you a quick, calm way to notice those thoughts and let them pass. It isn’t therapy or medical care, and it’s not a replacement for professional support.

Anyone who has decided they want to change their relationship with their thoughts, across every age, background and belief. Many people use it alongside a mindfulness or spiritual practice. No experience needed.
