What is Lumanta?

A simple thought-awareness tool.

Catch it. Tap.

Lumanta is a simple thought-awareness tool that helps you notice patterns in your thinking.

When you notice a Negative, Fear-Based, or Judgmental thought, simply catch it and tap.

  • No journaling.
  • No analyzing.
  • No judging yourself.

Over time, Lumanta makes those patterns visible, helping you create awareness, release what no longer serves you, and Free Up Space for other ways of thinking.
